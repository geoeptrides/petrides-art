PAH-006 · 2025 · International

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artResearch gapIncludes null findingsIncludes unfavourable findings

Finding. The healthcare review includes reports of no detectable benefit and some less favourable responses to visual-art interventions.

Limitation. Lack of reported adverse clinical events does not establish universal safety. Proposed explanations such as siting and consultation remain hypotheses.

Foster MW, Sanhueza C, Bahr E, et al. PLoS One. 2025;20(8):e0328215.

Methods and verification
