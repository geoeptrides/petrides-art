Collecting a Petrides work

Explore the work, understand its making and speak directly with the studio about the example you would like to acquire.

How do I purchase?

Choose “Enquire about this work” on a sculpture or work-on-paper page. The studio will confirm availability, discuss the available example and provide a written quotation for delivery to your address. Your enquiry does not reserve the work or commit you to purchase.

What does the edition number mean?

Each work is offered within a declared numbered edition. Artist’s proofs, shown as APs, are listed separately from the numbered edition. The individual work page states both quantities.

Artist’s proofs are retained by the studio for the archive and for museum placement.

What arrives with the work?

Each work is accompanied by a certificate of authenticity signed by George Petrides and care instructions for its specific material and finish. Sculptures travel in padded, fitted packaging; works on paper are supplied unframed, packed flat between rigid boards.

Delivery, insurance and returns

Timing. Sculptures are made to order: each example is finished in the studio after your order and dispatched within 6–8 weeks, or within 10 working days of payment if one is ready. Works on paper are printed to order and dispatched within 10 working days of payment. Your quotation confirms the date.

Delivery. Works are dispatched from Athens or Long Island City, New York. Delivery is quoted in writing for your address before you commit; for sculpture, mention any access, placement or installation requirements when you enquire.

Insurance. Every shipment is insured for the full value of the work until it is delivered. The cost is included in your delivery quotation.

Damage in transit. If a work arrives damaged, email studio@petrides.art within 7 days of delivery with photographs of the work and its packaging. The studio will repair or replace the work, or refund it in full, at no cost to you.

Returns. You may return a work within 14 days of delivery if it is in the condition in which it arrived. Return shipping and insurance are paid by the buyer, and the studio refunds the purchase price within 14 days of the work’s return.

What is the full cost?

Prices are shown in USD. Delivery and insurance, any applicable sales tax or VAT, and any import duties depend on your destination; they are set out in your written quotation before you commit. Payment is by bank transfer or card, as stated on the invoice, and the work is dispatched once payment has cleared.

Can I see the work in person?

Private appointments can be arranged at the studio in Athens or Long Island City, New York. Contact the studio with the work you would like to see and your preferred dates.
