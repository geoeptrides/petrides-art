PAH-048 · 2026 · Greece

Findings, methods and sources

Policy & contextPolicy / contextContext / synthesis

Finding. Programme and conference accounts describe a national referral-based arts initiative involving cultural institutions.

Limitation. Facilitated participation is outside core scope. Conference-reported treatment outcomes are not used here as evidence for permanent sculpture or murals.

Stefanis N. Presentation to the EU Culture and Health webinar "Scaling up Arts on Prescription Models", June 2026; Ministry of Culture / UMHRI programme documentation, artonprescription.gr.
