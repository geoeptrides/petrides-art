The WHO arts-and-health review has a broader scope

Research record PAH-009 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-009 · 2019 · WHO European Region

Findings, methods and sources

Policy & contextReviewContext / synthesis

Finding. The 2019 WHO review covers receptive and participatory activities across prevention, health promotion and treatment.

Limitation. Its overall findings cannot be presented as evidence for murals or sculpture alone. Art-making and arts therapies are outside this site's core scope.

Fancourt D, Finn S. What is the evidence on the role of the arts in improving health and well-being? A scoping review. Copenhagen: WHO Regional Office for Europe; 2019. Health Evidence Network synthesis report 67.
