Heroines of 1821

War Museum, Athens

For the War Museum in Athens.

Heroines of 1821 honors the women of the Greek War of Independence in the 1820s. The sculptor’s wife, Eleftheria (“Liberty”) Gkoufa, posed for the work in 2021; it seeks to convey strength, resilience and defiance.
