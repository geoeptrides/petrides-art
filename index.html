Thomas Jefferson

A sculptural portrait by George Petrides

Donated to the U.S. Department of State for the Ambassador’s residence, marking the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence.

Sculpture completed and collected for shipment; installation pending. Delivery notes.

Jefferson was the first Secretary of State. His correspondence with Adamantios Korais connects the portrait to Greece’s own struggle for independence.

Steps to installation and dedication
1The sculpture is with the shipper in New York. Transport to Athens follows once the import documents are issued; security clearance by the Embassy.
2Embassy to confirm the recommended placement (inside the gate from Panagi Kyriakou Street).
3Review of the suggested construction by Embassy staff and engineer.
4Order the plaque and have it shipped to Athens.
5Landscaping team to clear the area.
6Construction of the base, as suggested or as modified by staff and engineer.
7Installation of the sculpture on the base (let George Petrides know if you would like him to be present for attaching the sculpture to the base).
8Landscaping team to design and finish the landscaping and paving around the sculpture.
9Dedication event.

Who does what

U.S. Embassy, AthensTransport to Athens and security clearance; the location decision; plaque text approval and ordering; engineering review; base construction by the local contractor; lighting, facilities and security approvals; installation; final landscaping; the dedication event.
Petrides Public SculptureThe base specification and the construction guidance on this website; confirming the base location on site before excavation; help with installation in Athens at no charge, including confirming the sculpture’s orientation and completing the final surface touch-ups; object documentation (signature and marks, dated condition report).

Costs

George Petrides has donated the sculpture. As set out in the briefing of 11 August 2026, the Embassy bears the other project costs: transport from New York to Athens, base construction, plaque fabrication, lighting, landscaping and any dedication event. George Petrides’s help with the installation in Athens is at no charge.
