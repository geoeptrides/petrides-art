Nature views, artwork content and nurse burnout

Research record PAH-061 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-061 · 2021 · Not recorded

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. The share of perceived nature views was among the strongest predictors of emotional exhaustion and depersonalisation scores. Artwork content was included as an explanatory variable but was not among the leading predictors reported.

Limitation. Small cross-sectional sample; primarily evidence about nature views rather than artwork.

Mihandoust S, Pati D, Lee J, Roney J. Exploring the relationship between perceived visual access to nature and nurse burnout. HERD. 2021;14(3):258–273.
