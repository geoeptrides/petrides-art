Student sculpture maquettes exhibited in a hospital museum

Research record PAH-077 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-077 · 2026 · Japan

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artDescriptive surveyContext / synthesis

Finding. A month-long exhibition in March 2025 showed sculpture maquettes by second-year sculpture students at the YHRP museum inside Hiroshima University Hospital. A questionnaire was completed by 37 people: 13 hospital users, 9 companions or family members, 3 hospital staff, 5 students, 5 members of the public and 2 others.

Limitation. A temporary exhibition of maquettes, not permanent installed sculpture. A descriptive questionnaire of a self-selected audience, with no comparison. Findings beyond the abstract and respondent table await a checked translation of the methods and results.

Hitokuwada T. 病院における彫刻・立体造形作品の可能性と課題に関する研究II [Research on the possibilities and tasks of sculptures and three-dimensional works in hospitals II]. Studies in Art Education (美術教育学研究). 2026;58(1):177–184. doi:10.19008/uaesj.58.177
