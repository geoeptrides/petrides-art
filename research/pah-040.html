A nature mural with sound during bronchoscopy

Research record PAH-040 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-040 · 2003 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceRandomised trialMixed findingsIncludes null findings

Finding. A trial of 80 adults found better rated pain control with a nature mural plus nature sounds; anxiety did not significantly differ.

Limitation. A combined audiovisual intervention. The separate effect of the mural cannot be determined.

Diette GB, Lechtzin N, Haponik E, Devrotes A, Rubin HR. Distraction therapy with nature sights and sounds reduces pain during flexible bronchoscopy: a complementary approach to routine analgesia. Chest. 2003;123(3):941–948.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Structured summary

Population
Consecutive adults undergoing flexible bronchoscopy with conscious sedation, teaching hospital in Baltimore
Sample
80 patients
Intervention
Nature scene mural at the bedside plus recorded nature sounds before, during and after the procedure
Comparator
Neither mural nor sounds
Outcome measures
Patient ratings of pain control (5-point scale) and anxiety
Timing
Around the procedure
Result
Greater odds of better pain control (OR 4.76, 95% CI 1.35–16.7); no difference in anxiety (OR 0.87, 95% CI 0.39–1.96)
Uncertainty
Wide confidence interval; combined audiovisual intervention
Design
Randomised controlled trial
Sample / scope
80 adults undergoing flexible bronchoscopy
Setting
Procedure room
Art form
Murals
