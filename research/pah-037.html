Public Art & Health

Nature art in two emergency waiting rooms

Research record PAH-037 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-037 · 2012 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. Before-and-after observations reported reductions in several restless behaviours after still and video nature art were introduced.

Limitation. No concurrent control site. A combined visual intervention and observed behaviour, rather than a direct clinical outcome.

Nanda U, Chanaud C, Nelson M, Zhu X, Bajema R, Jansen BH. Impact of visual art on patient behavior in the emergency department waiting room. J Emerg Med. 2012;43(1):172–181.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Structured summary

Population
Patients and visitors in emergency department waiting rooms at two hospitals
Sample
Thirty hours of observation before and after installation at each site
Intervention
Still and video art depicting nature
Comparator
Same waiting rooms before installation
Outcome measures
Systematic behavioural observation: restlessness, noise, staring, front-desk queries, social interaction
Timing
Four months
Result
Significant reductions in restlessness, noise and staring at both sites; fewer front-desk queries and more social interaction at one site
Uncertainty
Pre–post design without a concurrent control site
Design
Pre–post systematic behavioural observation at two hospitals
Sample / scope
Thirty hours of observation before and after installation at each site
Setting
Emergency department waiting room
Art form
Pictures
