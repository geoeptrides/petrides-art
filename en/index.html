George Petrides · Sulālat al-Mustaqbal · Lineage of the Future

One family. A century of change.

What do we inherit from our families, and what do we choose to pass on?

Six larger-than-life sculptural portraits, accompanied by documentary films and archival footage, trace a family’s journey from Smyrna (now İzmir) to Athens to New York.
