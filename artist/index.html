George Petrides

George Petrides is a sculptor working between Athens and New York. His figurative work explores family memory, cultural inheritance and the human experiences carried across generations.

Beginning in clay, his practice moves through digital development and specialist fabrication to the finished sculpture. The editions bring the subjects and textures of that practice into a scale for everyday encounters.

Education and practice

Petrides studied Classics at Harvard College, graduating in 1985, and completed graduate studies at Stanford University in 1993. He studied and practised art alongside a career in international finance before committing fully to his artistic practice in 2017. His studies at the New York Studio School began in 2001; he earned its Certificate in Sculpture in 2022.
