Psychologically supportive design in a Jordanian public hospital

Research record PAH-073 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-073 · 2022 · Jordan

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalContext / synthesis

Finding. Proposes a framework of psychologically supportive design features drawn from healing-environment theory and finds the studied hospital moderately supportive, with connection to nature the weakest aspect.

Limitation. Single hospital; the framework covers design broadly and does not isolate artwork.

Al-Bqour N, Rababeh S, Al-Rabady R. The psychological supportive design features in hospitals: case of a public Jordanian hospital in Amman. HERD. 2022;15(1):173–188.
