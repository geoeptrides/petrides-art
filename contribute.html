Prepare a contribution

Complete the relevant fields, review the prepared entry, then send it with your email app or download a copy. This form does not send or store submissions on the website. Nothing is submitted until you send the email to research@publicart.health.

Do not include patient information or confidential material. Public source links are preferred. If sending a photograph separately, include the photographer’s credit and permission to publish. Contact details are for editorial follow-up and are not part of the public record. Your email provider handles the message when you send it. Entries are lost if you leave without downloading a copy.

Review before sending

Check the content, then open your email app and send it yourself. If no email app is configured, copy the text into webmail or attach the downloaded file to an email to research@publicart.health. Longer entries must be copied or attached. Suggestions are reviewed before publication; preparing a contribution does not add it to the public review queue.

An installed work

The installation register documents sculpture and other three-dimensional works permanently placed in healthcare settings. Paintings, murals and photographs remain within the scope of the research library but are not yet recorded in the installation register.

Required

  • Artist, title, institution, city and country
  • Year installed, or “not known”
  • Setting (indoor or outdoor) and form or material

Optional

  • A photograph. If you supply one, include the photographer’s name and permission to publish it here; verified records are published without photographs when none is available.
  • Dimensions, commissioning route, consultation, any evaluation and public sources

Institutions without consultation or evaluation data are welcome to submit: those fields will read “Not published”. The same standard already applies to most records in the register.
