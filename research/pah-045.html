Public Art & Health

Cultural attendance and mortality in older adults

Research record PAH-045 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-045 · 2019 · United Kingdom

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. A longitudinal cohort associated more frequent cultural attendance with lower subsequent mortality after statistical adjustment.

Limitation. Attendance included theatre and concerts as well as museums and galleries. Observational findings cannot establish causality or a benefit from installed art.

Fancourt D, Steptoe A. The art of life and death: 14 year follow-up analyses of associations between arts engagement and mortality in the English Longitudinal Study of Ageing. BMJ. 2019;367:l6377.
