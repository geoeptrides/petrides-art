The gap in evidence specific to hospital sculpture

Research record PAH-007 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-007 · 2025 · International

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artResearch gapContext / synthesis

Finding. The reviewed literature does not establish a causal health effect for permanent hospital sculpture isolated from its surroundings.

Limitation. This is a bounded conclusion about the material reviewed, not a claim that no sculpture research exists anywhere. See PAH-050 and PAH-055.

Foster MW, Sanhueza C, Bahr E, et al. PLoS One. 2025;20(8):e0328215.
