Aretaieia

Aretaieion University Hospital
Historical referenceWomen’s health at Aretaieion: gynecological care since 1906; independent University Gynecology Clinic established in 1922

Aretaieia stands at the entrance of Greece’s first university hospital, across Vasilissis Sofias Avenue from the U.S. Embassy. The over-life-size figure honors the hospital’s long tradition of women’s health: gynecological care at Aretaieion dates to 1906, with an independent University Gynecology Clinic established in 1922.

It is made from PETG recycled from discarded medical packaging, a material chosen for a hospital commission and designed for permanent outdoor installation.

Case study

Aretaieion University Hospital

Purpose
To honor Aretaieion’s long tradition of women’s health: gynecological care at the hospital dates to 1906, and its independent University Gynecology Clinic to 1922.
Figure, material and scale
An over-life-size figure, 220 cm high without its base, in a gold patina and metal coating over PETG recycled from discarded medical packaging, a material chosen for a hospital and for permanent outdoor display.
Setting and audience
At the entrance of Greece’s first university hospital, on Vasilissis Sofias Avenue opposite the U.S. Embassy, seen daily by patients, staff and passers-by.
Studio’s role
Modeled in clay, 3D-scanned and refined digitally, printed at scale and hand-finished in metals and patinas in the Petrides studio.
Relationship
Installed at Aretaieion University Hospital, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (2024).
Independent record
The National Herald: “Aretaieion Hospital Welcomes a Public Sculpture by George Petrides”
Institutional reference
“This project was not an easy one. Over three years, it involved much stakeholder consultation, multiple board approvals, several permits from the Hellenic Ministry of Culture, and, of course, fabrication by George to the highest possible technical and artistic standards in his studio in New York. He was involved at every step—flying to Athens for critical meetings, overseeing installation, and speaking at the dedication ceremony. His professionalism and patience throughout the many stages of such a complex project were extraordinary. I recommend him without reservation.”
Prof. Nikolaos Vlahos, MD, PhD, Chairman, 2nd Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Aretaieio University Hospital, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. Letter of 20 June 2025.
