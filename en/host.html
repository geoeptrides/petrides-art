What the exhibition brings

Six larger-than-life family portraits, historical films, archival footage, and interpretive texts follow a century from Smyrna to Athens to New York. Arabic is the exhibition’s principal interpretive language; the seven films, this website, and the downloadable introduction are also available in English.

Visitors can move around the portraits, follow the family’s history through the accompanying texts, and watch the introduction, five portrait films and the making film. The sculpture arrangement and film-viewing area are planned together for each venue.

Space and layout

The six busts measure 75–91 cm in height and 55–60 cm in diameter, excluding display bases. The layout combines views around each sculpture, accessible circulation, interpretive texts and a film-viewing area. Share a floor plan and photographs to discuss an arrangement suited to your venue.

Regional context for host institutions

The table below sets out the exhibition’s connections with Gulf heritage and with the national visions of Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar, for use in hosting proposals.

Show the values and references
ValueIn the exhibitionCultural reference
Family and careThe Haven and The Companion recognise the daily care through which a family endures.Saudi Vision 2030 commits to strengthening families as a foundation of a vibrant society. [1]
Respect for elders and ancestryMaria anchors four generations. The portraits give ancestors a visible place in the family’s story.UNESCO describes Gulf majlis elders as bearers of genealogy and history, passing knowledge to the young. [2]
Heritage carried forwardThe Crucible gives inherited memories a new form. Hand modelling and digital fabrication join tradition with contemporary tools.Qatar National Vision 2030 calls for a balance between modernisation and the preservation of tradition. [3]
Responsibility to future generationsSofia’s upward gaze turns to the next generation. Recycled PETG gives discarded material a new use.The UAE’s Year of the Family 2026 highlights family cohesion and the passing of values to future generations. [4]
Dialogue across culturesA Greek family story, shared in Arabic and English, invites Gulf audiences to recognise experiences across cultures.Qatar National Vision 2030 supports intercultural understanding and cultural heritage alongside diversity. [5]
