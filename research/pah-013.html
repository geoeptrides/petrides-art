Percent-for-art as a funding route

Research record PAH-013 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-013 · 2025 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Policy & contextPolicy / contextContext / synthesis

Finding. Some jurisdictions allocate part of eligible public-construction budgets to art; hospital projects may qualify.

Limitation. Rules vary. The available sources do not establish that this is the predominant funding mechanism for hospital art nationwide.

National Assembly of State Arts Agencies, state percent-for-art programs; New York City Department of Cultural Affairs, Percent for Art.
