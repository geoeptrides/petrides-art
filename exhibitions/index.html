2022–2027 · Eight cities

Hellenic Heads: Legacy and Renewal

Six over-life-size busts connect six periods of Greek history with six members of the artist’s family. Hellenic Heads has reached eight cities since 2022, including Venice in parallel with Biennale Arte 2024. Its Berlin presentation is on view through 8 January 2027. The exhibition is accompanied by films, interpretive panels and an illustrated catalogue.

Planned for the Gulf

Sulala: Lineage of the Future

Six larger-than-life sculptural portraits trace four generations of one family, from Smyrna (now İzmir) to Athens to New York, with documentary films and archival footage.

The exhibition is planned for Qatar, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia, in Arabic and English. Venues will be announced.

London · 8 March 2027

Janus of Love

Two female faces joined back to back, one pensive and one looking forward, reflect on love’s endings and beginnings. Made for International Women’s Day, the work will be exhibited on 8 March 2027 at the residence of the Ambassador of Greece in London. Concept: Silvia Schwarzer.

The six busts of Hellenic Heads

Thalia · Classical Greece
Panayota Papaioannou, the artist’s mother. After the Vatican’s Thalia, Muse of Comedy.

Archon · Byzantine Empire
Captain Christos Petrides, the artist’s father. After the colossal Constantine in Rome’s Capitoline Museums.

Heroines · Greek War of Independence
Eleftheria Gkoufa, the artist’s wife. Honoring three women who took leadership roles in the Struggle; after Charles Cordier’s Saïd Abdullah.

The Refugee · Asia Minor Catastrophe
Maria Blizioti, the artist’s maternal grandmother, at nineteen on her arrival in Piraeus. After Michelangelo’s Rondanini Pietà.

Man of Two Wars · Occupation and Civil War
A self-portrait. After Rodin’s Pierre de Wissant from The Burghers of Calais.

Kore · Modern Greece
Sofia Petrides, the artist’s daughter, who posed at twelve. After Kore 684 in the Acropolis Museum, Athens.
