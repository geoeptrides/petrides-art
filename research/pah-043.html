Public Art & Health

Brief art viewing and blood pressure in a museum

Research record PAH-043 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-043 · 2019 · Italy

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. A small experiment compared responses to figurative art, modern art and an office setting, reporting a systolic blood-pressure reduction in the figurative condition.

Limitation. Healthy museum participants, not hospital patients. One experiment cannot determine a universal preference or clinical art-selection rule.

Mastandrea S, Maricchiolo F, Carrus G, Giovannelli I, Giuliani V, Berardi D. Visits to figurative art museums may lower blood pressure and stress. Arts Health. 2019;11(2):123–132.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Structured summary

Population
Museum visitors without hypertension
Sample
77 participants, randomly assigned
Intervention
Visit to figurative art or modern art
Comparator
Museum office
Outcome measures
Blood pressure and heart rate
Timing
Before and after the visit
Result
Systolic blood pressure decreased for most participants exposed to figurative art compared with modern art and the office; no heart-rate differences
Uncertainty
Small sample of healthy participants in a museum
Design
Randomised allocation, pre–post physiological measurement
Sample / scope
77 participants
Setting
Art museum (Rome)
Art form
Pictures
