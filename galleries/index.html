Representation and placement

For galleries and art advisors

George Petrides works with galleries and art advisors who place sculpture with private collectors, hotels and corporate collections. The studio welcomes both ongoing representation and collaboration on a single project.

Commissions

Site-specific sculpture for residences, hotels, headquarters and corporate collections. Commissions typically range from US$150,000 to US$1,000,000.

The record

40 public sculptures across seven sectors and eight countries, including four works for Tiffany & Co. in New York, Madrid, Toronto and Paris, and works for the Permanent Mission of Greece to the United Nations and the Ecumenical Patriarchate of Constantinople. The work has been covered by Forbes, Cultured, The Brooklyn Rail and Kathimerini.

Working with the studio

The studio provides images, specifications and availability on request, and coordinates fabrication, delivery and installation directly with the gallery or advisor. Terms are agreed for each engagement.
