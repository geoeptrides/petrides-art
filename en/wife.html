Wife: The Companion

The artist’s wife

The six portraits

The Companion is the partner with whom a family is built. Her portrait brings partnership and mutual support into the family’s story, and together the artist and his wife carry that story into a new generation.

Where the earlier portraits trace a line through the generations, The Companion turns towards partnership in the present. A copper veil frames the face and falls over the shoulders; against it, the worked surface of the face and the level, steady gaze give the relationship a quiet, concentrated presence.
