Selected works

Tiffany & Co. · The Landmark · New York

Head of Thalia II

For the reopening of The Landmark at 727 Fifth Avenue in 2023, Tiffany & Co., with Peter Marino, commissioned a work for the building’s art collection. Modelled in clay from photographs of the artist’s mother around 1950, it was cast in bronze near ancient Thebes and given a patina recalling Tiffany’s signature colour.

Children’s Hospital “Aglaia Kyriakou” · Athens

Kore II

This larger-than-life bust stands at the entrance of one of Greece’s largest children’s hospitals, with Sofia Petrides as the model. A variant, Kore III, is in the foyer of the Permanent Mission of Greece to the United Nations.

Jefferson House · Residence of the U.S. Ambassador, Athens

Jefferson in Athens

Thomas Jefferson studied classical Greek political thought and corresponded with Adamantios Korais of the Modern Greek Enlightenment. Made for the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence, the head draws on four period portraits held to be among his truest likenesses.

Saint George Square · Neo Psychiko, Athens

Refugee-Woman of Smyrna

Refugee-Woman of Smyrna stands in Neo Psychiko, Athens. Modelled on the sculptor’s maternal grandmother, it recalls the catastrophe of Smyrna in September 1922 and connects a public place with the family history at the heart of this exhibition: the same grandmother appears here as Grandmother: The Root.

Presidential Gift · The White House, Washington

Davy Crockett at the Alamo

This tabletop sculpture shows Davy Crockett in the final assault on the Alamo on 6 March 1836: frontiersman, congressman and folk hero, among the ruined walls on a destroyed 18-pounder cannon, holding a torch and a Bowie knife. The White House accepted it as a Presidential Gift in 2025.
