Cortisol after a lunchtime gallery visit

Research record PAH-044 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-044 · 2006 · United Kingdom

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. A small uncontrolled study reported lower cortisol and self-reported stress after a gallery visit.

Limitation. Time of day, the break from work and other factors were not controlled. The linked 2019 review is a secondary account of Clow and Fredhoi's 2006 study.

Clow A, Fredhoi C. Normalisation of salivary cortisol levels and self-report stress by a brief lunchtime visit to an art gallery by London City workers. Journal of Holistic Healthcare. 2006;3(2):29–32. Secondary account: Mastandrea et al. (2019).
