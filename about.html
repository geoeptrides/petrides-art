About & disclosure

About this repository

An artist-founded resource for examining research on installed visual art in healthcare.

Purpose

The resource is for healthcare leaders, clinicians, architects, artists, funders and researchers considering permanent art in healthcare. It focuses on the experience of encountering art that is already present in the environment.

Founder and declared interest

The repository was founded by sculptor George Petrides, whose practice includes public sculpture in healthcare settings.

His works at Aretaieion University Hospital, Aglaia Kyriakou Children’s Hospital, Tzaneio Hospital, Elena Venizelou Hospital and Alexandra Hospital appear as labelled examples of practice. Their inclusion is not evidence of health benefits, and no health-outcome claim is made for those works. Any independent research concerning them is assessed separately under the same published criteria, with the relationship disclosed.

The founder has a professional interest in public sculpture and its placement in healthcare. This interest is disclosed so readers can assess the resource accordingly. The repository does not claim independent institutional governance or external peer review.

Editorial responsibility

The founder is the publisher and is responsible for the repository’s content. The roles below will be filled only with people who have agreed to take them on; each entry will give the person’s relevant qualifications and the scope of what they maintain, assess or review.

Funding and editorial control

The repository is funded entirely by its founder, George Petrides, personally. Editorial control rests with the publisher, subject to the declared interest above. No external funder, sponsor or institution has editorial control over the content, and no partnership is implied by any source cited.

Independent review

The repository is inviting researchers in arts and health, environmental psychology, epidemiology and healthcare design to review its methods and records. Reviewers will be named here only with their consent, together with the scope of what each has reviewed. Until then, the declared interest above applies in full and no external review is claimed.

To suggest a correction, a record or an installation, see Contribute a record.

Editorial position

Inclusion does not depend on a positive finding. Positive, mixed and null results receive the same record structure and visual treatment, with limitations displayed alongside findings. No numerical confidence score is assigned without a documented appraisal method.

Artistic quality, cultural meaning and the experience of a place are legitimate institutional concerns. They should be described on their own terms, with clinical claims reserved for findings that the evidence supports.

References to universities, hospitals and research groups identify sources. They do not imply affiliation, endorsement or partnership. This resource does not provide art therapy or medical treatment.

Contact and contributions

research@publicart.health

For a proposed research entry, include the citation, primary-source link, population, intervention and outcome. For an installation, include artist, title, institution, date and location. A photograph is optional; if you supply one, include the photographer’s credit and permission to publish it.
