Staff and visitors remain less studied than patients

Research record PAH-005 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-005 · 2025 · International

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artResearch gapContext / synthesis

Finding. The 2025 scoping review found substantially more patient research than research on staff or visitors.

Limitation. A description of research coverage, not evidence that staff benefit less. Visitors, rather than staff, form the smallest named participant group.

Foster MW, Sanhueza C, Bahr E, et al. PLoS One. 2025;20(8):e0328215.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Full text assessed

Evidence about the literature, drawn from a scoping review
