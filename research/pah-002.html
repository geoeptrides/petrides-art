Nature and abstract images after cardiac surgery

Research record PAH-002 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-002 · 1993 · Sweden

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artRandomised trialConference abstractReported benefit

Finding. A frequently cited 1993 conference abstract reported differences in anxiety and analgesic use between image conditions.

Limitation. Full trial reporting was not located. This abstract cannot establish a general rule against abstract art.

Ulrich RS, Lundén O, Eltinge JL. Effects of exposure to nature and abstract pictures on patients recovering from heart surgery. Paper, 33rd meeting of the Society for Psychophysiological Research, Rottach-Egern. Abstract in Psychophysiology. 1993;30(Suppl 1):7.
