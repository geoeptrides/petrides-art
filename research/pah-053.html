PAH-053 · 2025 · Germany

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. A field study reports changes in neighbourhood connectedness and wellbeing after engagement with a public exhibition.

Limitation. Adjacent public-space evidence. The original record was based on the abstract; no hospital application was tested.

Kühnapfel C, Trupp MD, Pelowski M, Fingerhut J. On the impact of public art: how engaging a pedestrian-level exhibition improves neighborhood connectedness and well-being. Wellbeing Space Soc. 2025;8:100252. doi:10.1016/j.wss.2025.100252
