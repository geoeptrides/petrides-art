The subject

Half Bust of Thalia is a further work on paper from Petrides’s Thalia series. The subject connects the artist’s family memory with his continuing exploration of the human figure.

Paper, printing and presentation

A fine art inkjet print of George Petrides’s photograph of the sculpture, on Hahnemühle Torchon, a 285 gsm paper with a textured felt surface that is acid- and lignin-free and meets the ISO 9706 standard for age resistance. Each print is numbered within an edition of nine plus one artist’s proof and supplied unframed, packed flat between rigid boards. The studio can advise on framing.

Delivery and care

Delivery is quoted in writing for your address before you commit and is insured for the full value of the work until it reaches you. You may return a print within 14 days of delivery.
