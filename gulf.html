Gulf and Middle East

What the repository documents for the region, what the research shows, and where the gaps are. Evidence from the region is still limited, and this page says so plainly.

Qatar: Sidra Medicine, Doha

Outside. Damien Hirst’s The Miraculous Journey: fourteen bronzes charting human development before birth, along the entrance road of a hospital for women and children. They were covered shortly after their 2013 unveiling and shown again in November 2018. See installation record W-09.

Inside. Qatar Foundation reports more than sixty artworks, including models, paintings and interactive works (Qatar Foundation, 2022; PAH-075). The individual works are not listed publicly.

The building. Designed by Pelli Clarke & Partners around three atriums inspired by the sails of traditional dhows (Visit Qatar). A contractor’s case study describes the atriums as indoor healing gardens visible from the patient rooms (Midland Lead).

What is not known. How the interior collection was selected, whether patients or staff were consulted, and whether its effects have been evaluated.

United Arab Emirates: Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi

Cleveland Clinic states that its art collection extends to its Abu Dhabi hospital (Cleveland Clinic). The architects describe interiors that integrate arabesque patterns and regional colour palettes to give patients a sense of familiarity (HDR; PAH-076).

A practical example: during the pandemic, paintings donated by students for the isolation units were laminated so they could be disinfected before entering the hospital and during routine cleaning (Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, 2020). Infection control is a real constraint on art in clinical areas.

Research from the region

Research library

PAH-074 · Saudi Arabia · 2025

What families and staff value in inpatient rooms

In a survey of 359 patients, family visitors and staff in three hospitals, respondents preferred single rooms, natural light, comfortable seating and a family zone by the window with views of nature.

Does not address artwork.

PAH-073 · Jordan · 2022

Psychologically supportive design

A framework applied to a public hospital in Amman found it moderately supportive, with connection to nature the weakest aspect.

One hospital; art is not isolated.

PAH-072 · Libya · 2022

Preferences for rooms, including art

Participants preferred rooms with art and bright colours, window views and personalisation, and linked them to satisfaction and happiness.

Stated preferences in a simulated design exercise.

Gaps

  • No published evaluation of permanently installed art in a Gulf hospital has been located.
  • No study has been located on how figurative, abstract or calligraphic works are received in healthcare settings in the region. Preference findings from elsewhere, such as patients’ preference for figurative art in Germany (PAH-059), may not transfer.
  • Commissioning processes at Gulf hospitals are largely undocumented in public sources.
  • The installation register holds one Gulf work; no sculptures in Saudi, Emirati, Kuwaiti, Omani or Bahraini hospitals have yet been documented.

For hospitals in the region considering art

These points follow from the records above and the commissioning guidance. They are considerations, not findings.

  • Plan for infection control from the start, especially in clinical areas.
  • Families are present and valued in the care environment (PAH-074); consider what they will see from where they sit.
  • Ask patients, families and staff what they would find meaningful, rather than assuming.
  • Build in evaluation, so that the region adds to the evidence rather than borrowing it. See the evaluation guide.

Hospitals, artists and researchers in the region are invited to contribute records.

ملخص باللغة العربية

يوثّق هذا المستودع البحثي الأعمال الفنية المثبّتة في مرافق الرعاية الصحية والأبحاث المتعلقة بها. وفي منطقة الخليج والشرق الأوسط، تبقى الأدلة محدودة.

  • سدرة للطب، الدوحة: تضم أربعة عشر عملاً برونزياً للفنان داميان هيرست بعنوان «الرحلة المعجزة» عند مدخل المستشفى، وتفيد مؤسسة قطر بأن المستشفى يضم أكثر من ستين عملاً فنياً في الداخل.
  • كليفلاند كلينك أبوظبي: تمتد مجموعة كليفلاند كلينك الفنية إلى مستشفى أبوظبي، ويصف المعماريون تصاميم داخلية تستلهم الزخارف العربية وألوان المنطقة.
  • الأبحاث: دراسات من السعودية والأردن وليبيا تتناول تفضيلات المرضى والعائلات والعاملين في تصميم الغرف، ومنها الإضاءة الطبيعية والإطلالة على الطبيعة والأعمال الفنية والألوان الزاهية.

لم نعثر حتى الآن على أي تقييم منشور لأثر الأعمال الفنية المثبّتة في مستشفيات الخليج، ولا على دراسة تبحث في كيفية تلقّي الأعمال التشخيصية أو التجريدية أو الخطّية في هذه البيئات. نرحّب بمساهمات المستشفيات والباحثين في المنطقة عبر البريد الإلكتروني research@publicart.health.
