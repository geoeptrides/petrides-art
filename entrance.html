Recommended placement

The front entrance.

I recommend the front entrance of Jefferson House, where the portrait would identify the residence and greet arriving guests.

Advantages and considerations

From the project briefing of 11 August 2026.

Setting

The portrait continues the sculptural tradition of marking an important threshold with a commemorative figure, and would read clearly against the existing hedge.

Considerations

  • Visible primarily to arriving and departing guests, rather than from the house, patio or rear garden.

Positioning and site work

PositioningIn the planted bed shown above. Confirm the precise position on site with George Petrides before excavation; see installation arrangements for fitting and orientation.
LandscapeRemove one existing planting and trim the hedge in front of and around the base. Retain the existing double row of privacy hedges. Set a low planted bed around the base, with final planting by the Embassy.
LightingUse existing lighting supports near the gate, subject to facilities review and quotation.
IrrigationNo relocation is expected based on the June–July 2026 site study; the Embassy’s contractor should confirm this on site.
