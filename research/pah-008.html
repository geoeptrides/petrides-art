Healthcare design provides context for art research

Research record PAH-008 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-008 · 2008 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Policy & contextReviewContext / synthesis

Finding. Research on hospital design examines lighting, acoustics, ventilation and room configuration, among other variables.

Limitation. Effects of these building features cannot be assigned to an artwork within the building.

Ulrich RS, Zimring C, Zhu X, et al. A review of the research literature on evidence-based healthcare design. HERD. 2008;1(3):61–125.
