Nature-themed window murals in paediatric rooms

Research record PAH-023 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-023 · 2019 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artRandomised trialReported benefit

Finding. A study of 90 paediatric patients compared aquatic murals, tree murals and rooms without murals, reporting differences in physiological measures.

Limitation. Small, single-site study. Its results do not establish that every mural or subject will reduce heart rate or blood pressure.

Pearson M, Gaines K, Pati D, Colwell M, Motheral L, Adams NG. The physiological impact of window murals on pediatric patients. HERD. 2019;12(2):116-129.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Structured summary

Population
Paediatric inpatients
Sample
90 patients
Intervention
Aquatic or tree-themed window mural in the room
Comparator
Room without a mural
Outcome measures
Heart rate and blood pressure from medical data
Timing
During the stay, from admission
Result
Improvements in heart rate and systolic blood pressure in mural rooms compared with control rooms; tree murals associated with the most outcomes
Uncertainty
Effect sizes not given in the abstract; single site
Design
Patients randomly assigned to one of three room conditions; physiological measurement
Sample / scope
90 paediatric patients; see paper for allocation and analysed sample
Setting
Paediatric inpatient
Art form
Murals
