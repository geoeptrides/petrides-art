Photography

Image credits.

Photographs are credited to their sources. Images document artworks and settings; their inclusion does not indicate endorsement by an artist or institution.

Rights holders. Photographs are displayed only where a licence or permission is recorded. Thirteen earlier photographs whose permission could not be verified were withdrawn on 28 September 2026 and are listed below as not displayed. If you hold rights in an image and would like it credited differently, licensed or removed, write to research@publicart.health. Requests are logged in the corrections record on the Methods page.

Where an explicit Creative Commons licence was identified, it is linked below. Other photographs retain their original rights; no transferable publication licence is asserted by this site. Founder photographs were supplied by the founder for publication on this site.
