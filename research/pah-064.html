PAH-064 · 2025 · Mexico

Findings, methods and sources

PracticePolicy / contextContext / synthesis

Finding. Describes IMSS’s collection of murals, sculptures and architectural works across its facilities as an expression of institutional identity and of the values of Latin American social medicine.

Limitation. Historical and documentary; no health outcomes are measured.

Pacheco-Zavala MDP, Hernández-Ordóñez R, Smolinski-Kurek RL. Murales, medicina y memoria: el patrimonio cultural del IMSS como generador de identidad institucional. Rev Med Inst Mex Seguro Soc. 2025;63(5):e6710.
