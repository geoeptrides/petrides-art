Reading the map

Paintings and pictures in patient rooms and waiting areas are the most studied form, including four of the randomised trials. Sculpture has 6 records, and none is a trial measuring health outcomes for a permanent hospital sculpture; see PAH-007. The two added in Edition 4.5 are a temporary exhibition of maquettes (PAH-077) and a multi-component programme (PAH-078). Much of what is known about sculpture comes from practice records, adjacent settings such as public plazas, and theory.

For hospital boards, this means sculpture should be commissioned for reasons it can support, such as orientation, identity, dignity and the quality of arrival, and evaluated where possible. The evaluation guide suggests how.
