Constantine and the Vision of the Cross (Reduction for His All-Holiness Patriarch Bartholomew)

Ecumenical Patriarchate of Constantinople
Historical referenceConstantine refounded ancient Byzantion and inaugurated it as an imperial capital in AD 330

Petrides was commissioned to create a presentation piece for the apostolic visit of His All-Holiness Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew to the United States. The gold work pictured here is held by the Patriarchate in Istanbul. The subject carries deep resonance: the Patriarchate is in present-day Istanbul, historic Constantinople, which Constantine refounded from ancient Byzantion and inaugurated as an imperial capital in AD 330.

The Patriarchate subsequently commissioned a second, larger version fabricated in recycled PETG, consistent with the longstanding environmental advocacy of His All-Holiness.
