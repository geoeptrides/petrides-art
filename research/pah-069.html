Improving environments for care at the end of life

Research record PAH-069 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-069 · 2011 · United Kingdom

Findings, methods and sources

PracticeProgramme / guidanceContext / synthesis

Finding. Describes nurse-led projects to improve environments for end-of-life care under the King’s Fund Enhancing the Healing Environment programme, including findings of an independent evaluation by the University of Nottingham.

Limitation. Environmental improvements broadly; the contribution of artwork is not isolated.

The King’s Fund. Environments for care at end of life: the King’s Fund’s Enhancing the Healing Environment Programme 2008–2010. London: The King’s Fund; 2011.
