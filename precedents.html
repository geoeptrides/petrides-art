A bronze sculpture associated with the hospital's longstanding connection to J. M. Barrie's Peter Pan. The hospital reported temporary relocation during building works in 2023.

Installed sculpture Source: institution or artist

W-07 · 2000; Tinker Bell added 2005
Installation record & sources
Date
2000; Tinker Bell added 2005
Material
Bronze
Scale
The Peter Pan figure is recorded as a four-foot statue (approximately 122 cm); Tinker Bell, on his raised hand, has been described as London's smallest statue
Setting
Exterior, outside the hospital on Great Ormond Street; temporarily relocated from November 2023 during construction of the Children's Cancer Centre, to return to the new main entrance
Commissioning

Consultation
Not published.

Present location not confirmed. The photograph, dated 11 February 2019, shows the former entrance setting.
