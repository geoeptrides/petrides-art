Public Art & Health

Room artwork improved perceptions, with no detected clinical benefit

Research record PAH-016 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-016 · 2018 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artRandomised trialMixed findingsIncludes null findings

Finding. In a randomised trial of 186 cancer inpatients, paintings improved perceptions of the hospital environment. Psychological and clinical outcomes did not differ across groups.

Limitation. A single-site trial of room paintings. The null outcomes are central to the result, not evidence that the environmental benefit is unimportant.

George DR, de Boer C, Hammer J, Hopkins M, King T, Green MJ. Evaluating the benefits of hospital room artwork for patients receiving cancer treatment: a randomized controlled trial. J Hosp Med. 2018;13(8):558-561. doi:10.12788/jhm.2915

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Structured summary

Population
Adult inpatients with cancer diagnoses at the Penn State Cancer Institute, Hershey, admitted for treatment requiring at least a three-day stay
Sample
186 enrolled, 2014–2016; three randomised groups
Intervention
A painting by a local artist in the patient’s line of vision, either chosen by the patient or randomly selected
Comparator
No painting
Outcome measures
Anxiety, mood, depression, sense of control; self-reported pain, quality of life and length of stay; perceptions of the hospital environment
Timing
Anxiety and control at baseline and before discharge; emotional state every 1–2 days; perceptions of the room once, before discharge
Result
No differences in psychological or clinical outcomes; both painting groups reported significantly better perceptions of the hospital environment
Uncertainty
Effect sizes and confidence intervals are not given in the abstract; the authors note that other room décor could not be fully controlled
Design
Randomised controlled trial
Sample / scope
186 inpatients
Setting
Inpatient room
Art form
Pictures
