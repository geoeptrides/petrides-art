The subject

Medusa is a subject George Petrides connects with the myths and maritime history of Piraeus, where his family comes from and where he spent his early years.

This medium-scale head is offered at 19.5 cm high in an edition of nine numbered works plus one artist’s proof.

Making and finish

Petrides develops his figures through clay modelling, 3D scanning and digital sculpting. Fabrication methods vary with the work, including bronze casting.

This edition is 3D-printed in the studio in 100% recycled PETG from George Petrides’s clay model, reworked by hand in epoxy clay, then finished with ground bronze and brass applied in resin, burnished and patinated.

Edition and authenticity

Edition of 9 + 1 artist’s proof. Each work is numbered within the edition and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity from George Petrides. Artist’s proofs are retained by the studio for the archive and for museum placement.

Delivery and care

Delivery is quoted in writing for your address before you commit and is insured for the full value of the work until it reaches you. Sculptures travel in padded, fitted packaging. If a work arrives damaged, the studio repairs, replaces or refunds it at no cost to you, and you may return a work within 14 days of delivery.

Care instructions for the material and finish accompany every edition, and the studio can advise on placement, lighting and plinths.
