Art viewing in healthcare: the 2025 scoping review

Research record PAH-004 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-004 · 2025 · International

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artReviewContext / synthesis

Finding. The review mapped 68 publications: 35 peer-reviewed and 33 grey-literature items. It describes promising patient-experience findings alongside mixed and null results.

Limitation. No formal critical appraisal or pooled effect estimate. The 2026 correction reports 534 visitors, not 32, within 6,006 participants.

Foster MW, Sanhueza C, Bahr E, et al. The effects of viewing visual artwork on patients, staff, and visitors in healthcare settings: a scoping review. PLoS One. 2025;20(8):e0328215. Correction: PLoS One. 2026;21(4):e0347506.

Methods and verification

Verification level: Abstract checked

Design
Scoping review (JBI / PRISMA-ScR), no critical appraisal
Sample / scope
68 publications; 6,006 participants (3,133 patients, 1,343 staff, 534 visitors, 996 other/unspecified)
Setting
Corridors, entrances, waiting areas, inpatient rooms
