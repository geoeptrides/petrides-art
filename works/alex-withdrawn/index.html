The subject

A figure folded into a compact, inward pose. Alex Withdrawn concentrates attention on the contours of the body and the texture of its surface. At 17 cm high, the cast-resin sculpture invites a close encounter.

The British sculptor Garth Evans visited Petrides’s studio in April 2019 and singled out Alex Withdrawn.

Making and finish

Petrides develops his figures through clay modelling, 3D scanning and digital sculpting. Fabrication methods vary with the work, including bronze casting.

This edition is cast in resin and finished by hand in the studio.

Edition and authenticity

Edition of 23 + 2 artist’s proofs. Each work is numbered within the edition and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity from George Petrides. Artist’s proofs are retained by the studio for the archive and for museum placement.

Delivery and care

Delivery is quoted in writing for your address before you commit and is insured for the full value of the work until it reaches you. Sculptures travel in padded, fitted packaging. If a work arrives damaged, the studio repairs, replaces or refunds it at no cost to you, and you may return a work within 14 days of delivery.

Care instructions for the material and finish accompany every edition, and the studio can advise on placement, lighting and plinths.
