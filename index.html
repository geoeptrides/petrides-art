What does the research show?

Research reports benefits for some aspects of people’s experience of healthcare environments, alongside mixed and null findings. Results depend on the artwork, setting and intervention. The reviewed evidence does not establish a causal health effect for permanent hospital sculpture on its own.

About this resource

Public Art & Health is an editorially curated resource, rather than a systematic review. It was founded by sculptor George Petrides, whose practice includes work in healthcare settings.
