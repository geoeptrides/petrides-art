Children’s preferences for ceiling-tile artwork

Research record PAH-058 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-058 · 2025 · Not recorded

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. Children generally reported feeling better when viewing painted ceiling tiles. Realistic nature, realistic animals and cartoon content, and bright colour tones, were preferred; younger children reported the best experience.

Limitation. One health system; self-report collected by nursing staff; no comparison condition.

Biddle KS, Boggs LM, Wolfe ML, et al. Healing ceilings: a collaborative exploration of pediatric preferences in ceiling tile artwork. J Pediatr Nurs. 2025;81:e96–e105.
