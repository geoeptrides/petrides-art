PAH-033 · 2024 · United States

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. The institution reports patient-perceived benefits from its collection; the published 2014 study is also catalogued at PAH-024.

Limitation. These records overlap. Self-reported perceptions are valuable but do not demonstrate reduced clinical pain or effects of a particular work.

Karnik M, Printz B, Finkel J. A hospital's contemporary art collection: effects on patient mood, stress, comfort, and expectations. HERD. 2014;7(3):60-77. Cleveland Clinic, Our Art Collection (2024 survey).
