Art viewing and wellbeing: the 2025 systematic review

Research record PAH-042 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-042 · 2025 · International

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceReviewMixed findingsIncludes null findings

Finding. Across 38 papers and 6,805 participants, convergent evidence was found for eudemonic wellbeing—meaning, purpose and personal development. Other wellbeing outcomes lacked strong support.

Limitation. Settings and activities varied. The authors call for stronger methods and provide RAARR reporting guidance.

Trupp MD, Howlin C, Fekete A, Kutsche J, Fingerhut J, Pelowski M. The impact of viewing art on well-being—a systematic review of the evidence base and suggested mechanisms. J Posit Psychol. 2025;20(6):978–1002. doi:10.1080/17439760.2025.2481041
