Athens roundtable

Early preparations are under way for a roundtable in Athens on art in healthcare settings. Date, venue and format are not yet fixed.

Purpose

To bring together hospital and health-system leaders, researchers, healthcare architects, artists and funders to discuss what the research does and does not show about installed art in healthcare, and what institutions need in order to commission, care for and evaluate it well.
