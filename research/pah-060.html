Waiting-area art and patient satisfaction in China

Research record PAH-060 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-060 · 2021 · China

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. Satisfaction with the visual art on the walls was one of the significant predictors of overall satisfaction, alongside sound level, the size of the waiting area and signage.

Limitation. Cross-sectional association; art is one of several environmental factors and its effect is not isolated.

Xuan X, Li Z, Chen X, Cao Y, Feng Z. Study of the physical environment of waiting areas and its effects on patient satisfaction, experience, perceived waiting time, and behavior in China. HERD. 2021;14(3):108–123.
