Patients and physicians choose different art for patient rooms

Research record PAH-059 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-059 · 2025 · Not recorded

Findings, methods and sources

Healthcare artObservational / experimentalContext / synthesis

Finding. Both patient groups chose figurative art more often (65–67%) than physicians chose for their patients (42%). Views with natural elements were preferred across groups.

Limitation. Stated preference among a fixed set of images, not a measured health effect.

Balandin A, Wallenfang M, Wagener K, et al. Comparison of art preferences in patient rooms between chronic pain patients, palliative care patients and physicians. Wien Med Wochenschr. 2025;175(9–10):237–244.
