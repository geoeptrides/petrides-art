Art installations and greenery in urban streets

Research record PAH-049 · Finding, limitations, sources and publication relationships.

Back to the research library

PAH-049 · 2024 · Austria

Findings, methods and sources

Adjacent evidenceObservational / experimentalReported benefit

Finding. A field study compares short-term responses to art and greenery in street installations, reporting favourable changes in wellbeing measures.

Limitation. Public-space evidence rather than healthcare evidence. Similar observed results do not establish statistical equivalence.

Mikuni J, Dehove M, Dörrzapf L, et al. Art in the city reduces the feeling of anxiety, stress, and negative mood: a field study examining the impact of artistic intervention in urban public space on well-being. Wellbeing Space Soc. 2024;7:100215.
