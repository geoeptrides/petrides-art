Publications and overlap

Why the record count is not a count of independent studies.

Record, publication and study

A record is an editorial summary. It may cite several sources, and one publication may appear in several records. A review contains other studies, while different papers may use the same participants. Counts in the library and evidence map refer to records, not independent studies or participants.

Scope of this check

This pass compared the citations already listed in the records, DOI/PubMed identifiers and source URLs. It is bibliographic reconciliation, not a new appraisal of effectiveness or a full-text re-review. Multi-source records and studies included within reviews still need further matching.
