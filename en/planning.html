Hosting planning checklist

This checklist guides initial planning. Final specifications and installation requirements are agreed with the studio for each venue. The published dimensions below exclude display bases.

Confirm before a loan agreement

Object records

Confirm height × width × depth, weight, work/version date and intended indoor or outdoor use.

Bases and stability

Agree base footprint and height, combined height, anchoring, stability and floor loading with the venue team.

Handling and transport

Confirm shipping origin, crate dimensions and weights, access, lifting equipment, handling method and condition reporting.

Visitor experience

Agree the layout, accessible circulation, viewing distances, interpretation, lighting and film-viewing area.

Film presentation

Confirm seven film titles, runtimes, language versions, caption tracks, screening rights, offline files, screens and audio.

Responsibilities and costs

Record transport, customs, insurance, equipment, staffing, installation/deinstallation dates and all cost allocations in the agreement.
