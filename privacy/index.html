Privacy notice

This notice explains how Petrides Editions uses the details you submit on this website.

Enquiries

When you send an enquiry, the studio receives your name, email address, delivery country, optional postcode and telephone number, and your message. These details are used to respond to your enquiry and to prepare any quotation. You also receive a confirmation of your enquiry by email.

Editions list

If you join the editions list, your email address is sent to the studio and added to its mailing list, used to send previews, release announcements and occasional news from the studio. You receive a confirmation email with a link to unsubscribe, and every mailing includes one. Enquiries are not added to the list unless you tick the optional box.

Service providers

Enquiries and sign-ups are sent by email directly to the studio’s mailbox, studio@petrides.art. This website is hosted by Vercel, which records anonymous visit statistics without cookies.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete the details the studio holds about you, email studio@petrides.art.
