The art collection inside Sidra Medicine, Doha

PAH-075 · 2022 · Qatar

Findings, methods and sources

PracticePolicy / contextContext / synthesis

Finding. Qatar Foundation reports that Sidra Medicine holds more than sixty artworks, including models, paintings and interactive works, and quotes an art specialist on art’s role in humanising hospitals.

Limitation. An institutional account, not an evaluation. The individual works are not listed in the source.

Qatar Foundation. How do Sidra’s art pieces improve healthcare? 13 June 2022.
